MegaWatt Games Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

MegaWatt Games (the "app") is developed by YOUR LLC OR DEVELOPER NAME, also known as Blockadoo ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Summary

We do not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and statistics (such as today's guesses, games played, wins, streaks, and settings) only on your own iPhone. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this information is removed.

Notifications

If you choose to turn on daily reminders, the app asks for permission to send notifications. These reminders are created and delivered by your device (local notifications). No information is sent to us or to any server to deliver them. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in your iPhone's Settings.

Sharing

If you use the Share button, the app creates a short text result (for example, a score and colored squares) and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. We do not receive it.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and we do not share any data with third parties.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
