Frequently asked questions

Why can I only play once a day?

Each game gives one puzzle per day, and it's the same for everyone. Come back tomorrow for the next one.

My streak disappeared. Why?

A streak continues when you win on consecutive days. Missing a day, or not solving a puzzle, resets it.

How do I turn daily reminders on or off?

Tap the bell icon on the main screen. If you turned notifications off for the app in iPhone Settings, turn them back on under Settings → Notifications → MegaWatt Games.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your iPhone. If you delete the app or switch phones, your stats won't carry over.
